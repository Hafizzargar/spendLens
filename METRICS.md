# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Metrics 📈

To evaluate the success of SpendLens as a product-led growth (PLG) vehicle, we track a specific set of funnel metrics.

## 🌟 The North Star Metric
**"Volume of AI Spend Audited ($)"**
* Why? This metric perfectly aligns user value (they found money) with our business value (we identified a company with budget). If we hit $10M in audited spend, we know we have captured a massive pool of high-intent B2B leads.

## 📉 Funnel Metrics

### 1. Landing Page -> Audit Start Rate (%)
* **Goal:** > 40%
* **Definition:** Percentage of unique visitors who interact with the form and add at least one tool.
* **Tactic:** The Cinematic Onboarding and instant visibility of the tool dropdown "above the fold" (70vh) were explicitly designed to maximize this metric.

### 2. Audit Completion Rate (%)
* **Goal:** > 85%
* **Definition:** Percentage of users who click "Run AI Audit" after adding tools.
* **Tactic:** We removed authentication and CAPTCHAs from the frontend flow to ensure zero friction.

### 3. Lead Capture Conversion Rate (%)
* **Goal:** > 5%
* **Definition:** Percentage of completed audits where the user voluntarily inputs their email to receive the report or book a consultation.
* **Tactic:** We place the lead capture form directly below the shocking "Potential Savings" number, capitalizing on high emotional intent.

### 4. K-Factor (Virality / Share Rate)
* **Goal:** > 0.2
* **Definition:** How many new users are generated by each existing user sharing their public URL (`/audit/[id]`).
* **Tactic:** We implemented dynamic OpenGraph tags and Twitter Cards. When a user pastes their URL into Slack or Twitter, it unfurls a beautiful card showing exactly how much money they saved, driving curiosity clicks.
